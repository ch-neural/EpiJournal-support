Turn daily signals into a journal you can review

EpiJournal helps you capture events, observations, and personal notes in a structured way—so you can review changes over time, spot patterns, and share context when needed.

Why EpiJournal

  • Privacy-aware by design: clarity on what data is used and why

Use cases

  • Lifestyle tracking: sleep, diet, exercise, habits
  • Personal observations: symptoms, triggers, notes
  • Projects and research notes: capture, organize, review
